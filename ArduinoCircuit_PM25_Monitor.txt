I2C Connections (PM 2.5 Sensor)
QWIIC yellow line (SCK) - A5 (Arduino SCL)
QWIIC blue line (SDI) - A4 (Arduino SDA)
QWIIC black line - GND
QWIIC red line - power (3.3V)

TFT Monitor Connections
using EYESPI Connector	https://learn.adafruit.com/1-8-tft-display/eyespi

EYESPI - Arduino
VIN - VIN
3.3V - LIT
GND - GND
RST - D9
TDC - D8
TCS - D10
MOSI - D11
SCK - D13

then EYESPI Connector to TFT Display via EYESPI cable
